## Introduction

Football (soccer) is one of the most popular sports in the world. The Federation Internationale de Football Association (FIFA), established in 1904, is the governing body of international football. FIFA overseas the World Cup and the 211 countries/territories that are eligible for qualification. The FIFA World Cup is often recognized for uniting people from all over the world through a shared passion for the sport. 

## Background

FIFA prides itself on promoting equality and fairness on and off football fields. By establishing strict regulations and providing financial support to all member associations, FIFA tries its hardest to prevent geopolitical affairs from negatively impacting the world of football. Despite FIFA's efforts, it could be argued that a country's economic status and infrastructure play a role in their national team's performance. 

## Why is This Comparison Necessary?

An analysis of FIFA World Cup statistics over time is necessary to identify potential correletions between a country's infrastructure and their performance in the World Cup. Important variables, such as team demographics, and country tenure of the sport is taken into account. This analysis can be used to identify any potential areas of improvement related to international football, raise awareness of hidden disparaties, and form predictions for upcoming World Cup tournaments. 

## What is Analyzed in This Comparison?

This analysis focuses on:
* National teams that have won the World Cup the most times
* Team demographics and the clubs/leagues of players
* Country infrastructure of winning national teams
